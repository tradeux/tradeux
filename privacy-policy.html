POE TradeUX Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

POE TradeUX does not collect, sell, or transmit personal information to the extension developer or to third-party analytics services.

Data stored by the extension

The extension uses Chrome's local storage permission to save settings, presets, and favorites on your device. This data is used only to provide the extension's features.

Website access

POE TradeUX runs only on supported Path of Exile 2 trade pages. It may request trade-stat data from the Path of Exile website in the context of the page you are using. The extension does not request access to your browsing history, cookies, credentials, or account session data.

Contact and source code

Questions and source code are available at github.com/tradeux/tradeux.
